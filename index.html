Validation before automation

Your first 15 days

Build only what customers prove they need.

The goal is not guaranteed revenue in 15 days. It is evidence: five people willing to try QuoteNudge, then an attempt to convert three into paying customers.

DAYS 1–2

Research + demo

Interview independent auto detailers and cleaners. Build the smallest believable workflow around unanswered estimates.

DAYS 3–5

Watch real behavior

Show the prototype to five prospects. Ask how many quotes go quiet and what makes follow-up difficult.

DAYS 6–10

Improve what matters

Refine message timing, templates, and workflow from observed friction—not from a feature wish list.

DAYS 11–15

Test willingness to pay

Invite real use and, if the evidence supports it, test a $5/month Pro plan. Track paid conversion separately from signups.

AFTER VALIDATION

Add the commercial foundation

Introduce accounts, a database, verified subscription events, cancellation handling, backups, and monitoring.

OPERATING RULE

No premature automation

Automate repeated work only after the manual process is useful, understandable, and commercially validated.
